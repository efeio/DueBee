🔐 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

DueBee is an iOS subscription tracking application designed to help you stay in control of your recurring expenses. We take your privacy very seriously.

📊 Data Storage

All subscription and budget information you enter is stored strictly on your local device and inside your own private iCloud account:

  • Subscription names and descriptions
  • Prices, currencies, and billing cycles
  • First billing dates and renewal schedules
  • Categories and custom icons
  • Monthly budget limits

☁️ iCloud Sync (CloudKit)

Your data syncs across your devices via Apple CloudKit using your personal iCloud account. This data is encrypted and securely stored on Apple servers under your exclusive control. We do not operate external servers, and we have no access to your data.

💱 Exchange Rates

To accurately display total expenditure across different currencies, the app anonymously fetches exchange rate data from open.er-api.com. No personal data, user identifiers, or device information is transmitted during these requests.

🚫 What We Never Collect

  • Personal identity or contact details
  • Location data or advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Analytics, crash telemetries, or behavioral tracking
  • Third-party tracker information

🤝 Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with any third parties. The app contains no advertisement SDKs and no third-party tracking libraries.
